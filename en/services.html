Our Services

Appliance installation

A new device deserves a proper installation. We take it from unboxing to final tuning, so you enjoy it from day one, with no cables in sight.

  • Wall-mounted TVs on secure brackets
  • Home cinema and sound systems with proper speaker placement
  • Hidden wiring and tidying of existing cables
  • Connection and setup of receivers, consoles and peripherals
  • A walkthrough before we leave, no unanswered questions

Sound coverage

At an event, the sound either goes unnoticed or ruins it. We set up, tune and supervise the sound coverage so that every word and every note reaches everyone clearly.

  • Weddings, christenings and social events
  • Speeches, presentations and school celebrations
  • Permanent audio installations in shops and dining spaces
  • Microphone setups without feedback
  • A technician present throughout, where needed
  • Sound and lighting equipment rentals for events

Device repair service

Before you throw away a device that "broke", it deserves a proper diagnosis. Many faults can be fixed for far less than the cost of a new purchase, and we tell you honestly before you decide.

  • Fault diagnosis with a cost estimate before every repair
  • Repairs of TVs, amplifiers and sound systems
  • Inspection and maintenance of antenna and CCTV installations
  • Upgrades of older systems instead of full replacement

A tip

Before you throw it away, ask us.

Describe the problem by phone or via the contact form. We can often tell you in advance whether the repair is worth it, asking costs nothing.

Antenna & satellite installations

Stable, clean reception on every TV in the house, no frozen picture, no channels lost to the wind. We measure the signal, install properly and prove it before we leave.

  • Installation and alignment of digital terrestrial antennas
  • Satellite systems and receiver setup
  • Multiswitches and signal distribution to multiple points
  • Signal amplifiers and troubleshooting of reception problems
  • Measured with instruments, not "by eye"

CCTV & alarm systems

The peace of mind of knowing what's happening at your place, from wherever you are. We design the system around your space, not around a "package", and hand it over fully configured on your phone.

  • Security cameras with a recorder and remote access
  • Alarm systems for homes, shops and warehouses
  • Phone notifications and live monitoring
  • Video door entry and access control
  • Maintenance and expansion of existing systems

Start small

Start with one room.

You don't need to gut the house to make it smart. We start with the lighting or the thermostat, you see how it works in practice, and we expand whenever you want.

Ask for a proposal for your space

Smart homes

Lights that switch off when you leave, heating that's warm when you arrive, sockets you control from afar. We install automations that simplify everyday life, and save energy.

  • Smart lighting and ambience scenes
  • Smart thermostats and heating control
  • Smart sockets, shutters and door phones
  • Voice control and a phone app
  • Integration with CCTV and the alarm in one unified system

If it involves sound, image or security, chances are we do it.
